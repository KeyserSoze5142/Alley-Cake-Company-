Good to know

Frequently Asked Questions

Where are you located?

We're at 412 22nd Ave, Northport, AL 35476 — in the alley way behind Billy's Sports Grill, just minutes from downtown Tuscaloosa. That's the "Alley" in Alley Cake Co.!

How far in advance should I order?

Please allow at least one week for all custom orders. For weddings and large events, reach out as early as you can — popular dates fill up fast.

How much does a custom cake cost?

Round cakes start at $49, tiered cakes at $195, and cupcakes are $32.50 per dozen. Pricing is a guide — the final price depends on materials, 3D elements and overall design complexity. See the full menu.

Do you make wedding cakes?

Absolutely — wedding cakes are one of our favorite things to create. We'll design your cake around your colors, flowers and theme. Get in touch to start planning.

How do I place an order?

Call us at (205) 345-8610, email alleycakecompany@gmail.com, or use our order request form and we'll get right back to you.
