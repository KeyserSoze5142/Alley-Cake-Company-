Hi, I'm Sarah

Art You Can Eat

“Art has always been a part of who I am.”

Sarah is an Alabama native who picked up a piping bag at 18 and discovered that cake was her canvas. More than 25 years later, that passion has grown into Sarah's Alley Cake Co. — a custom bakery where every cake, cookie and petit four is a small work of art.

Her decorating skill earned her a spot on Food Network's Cake Challenge, competing alongside some of the country's best cake artists. But her favorite place to create is right here at home, making cakes for Northport and Tuscaloosa families' biggest moments — first birthdays, graduations, weddings and everything in between.
